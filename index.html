Privacy policy

A clear look at how Lenden Bahi handles your information, and the choices you have over your records.

1. Information stored on your device

Lenden Bahi is an offline bookkeeping app. It stores the information you enter or select to provide your local ledger:

  • Your local User ID, password verifier and recovery-code verifier.
  • People’s names and contact details that you enter, financial transactions, balances, notes and audit history.
  • Documents you attach, generated reports, app settings and backup preferences.

Local accounts and their records are kept separately in the app’s private Android storage. An email address or phone number used as a User ID is not verified online.

2. How the app uses information

The app processes this information on your device to sign you into a local account, maintain your ledger, calculate balances, create reports, preserve transaction history and back up or restore your records.

The current version has no backend account service, advertising, analytics or cloud synchronization. It does not automatically send messages to borrowers or upload your ledger to the developer.

3. Sharing and file access

Records leave the app when you choose to save, share or print a PDF, CSV or reminder text. The receiving app, storage provider, recipient or printer handles those copies under its own practices.

Attachments are copied into the app only after you choose them through Android’s system file picker. Backup access is limited to the folder you select and grant access to. The app does not request access to your phone’s contacts, SMS, camera, microphone or location in this version.

The developer does not receive or sell your local ledger data.

4. Backups and security

You can select a device or SD-card folder for encrypted, per-account backup archives. These archives use a backup passphrase that you provide. Android Keystore protects the saved secret used for scheduled backups on that installation. Android may delay scheduled work.

Passwords and recovery codes are verified using salted hashes. The app’s private ledger and credentials are excluded from Android platform cloud backup. This version does not integrate Google Drive.

Keep your device, recovery code and backup passphrase secure. The developer has no remote copy of your ledger or universal recovery key. A backup restored after reinstall requires its backup passphrase. No storage or security measure provides an absolute guarantee.

5. Retention, access and deletion

You can review and export your records in the app. Logging out does not delete your account or ledger. Financial corrections preserve the original history and use reversal entries.

To remove all private local accounts and their data from this installation, use Android Settings → Apps → Lenden Bahi → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Android menu names may vary. Save a verified backup first if you want to recover the records later.

Uninstalling or clearing storage does not delete external backup archives, exported PDFs or CSVs, printed records or copies shared with other apps. Delete those separately from the places where you saved or shared them. Optional backup retention applies only to completed archives owned by the account in the selected folder.

Support cannot access or delete device-only data remotely.

6. About this policy website

This policy page has no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or embedded third-party assets. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the page involves a connection to GitHub, which may process technical information, including your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app’s offline ledger storage.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current offline version of Lenden Bahi (Android package com.mora.lendenbahi). If its data practices change, including the addition of online services, cloud backup, analytics or advertising, this page will be updated with a revised effective date.
